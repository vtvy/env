This guide is for developers who want to *understand* their environment, not just copy commands. By the end you'll be able to answer:

- Why does `java -version` in my terminal differ from what my IDE uses?
- Why did adding one tool to `PATH` break another?
- What's the difference between `nvm`, `pyenv`, and `mise` — and which should I use?
- How do I onboard a new dev in 10 minutes instead of 2 days?

## How to read this guide

1. **Concepts** — read in order. They build on each other.
2. **Install / Version managers / Shell** — pick your OS, follow the steps.
3. **Recipes** — full end-to-end walkthroughs for common stacks.
4. **AI prompts** — copy these into your AI coding agent to skip the manual steps.

## What "multi-environment" means here

Most developers juggle multiple Node versions, multiple Python versions, sometimes multiple JDKs. This guide assumes that's your reality and shows you how to make it painless.
