## Why this guide exists

Most setup guides give you commands without the *why*. You copy them, something breaks weeks later, and nobody can explain what changed. This guide does the opposite — it explains the layers first (system, user, process, IDE, project), then shows you the tools that fit each layer, then gives you copy-paste recipes per OS.
